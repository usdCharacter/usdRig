# GraphView layer map (issue #54, Phase 1)

Authoritative assignment of every method of `GraphView` in
`plugin/usdNoodles/graphView.py` to the target decomposition layers.
Generated from the AST of `graphView.py` (311 method defs, 308 unique
names; `nodeGraph`, `linksChanged`, and `linkSelectionMode` each carry a
getter and setter). LOC is the AST line span of each def; the LOC figures
in issue #54 counted logical regions including nested helpers.

Layers: `model`, `intents`, `editing`, `interaction`, `render`,
`selection`, `stage_sync`, plus `core` for the coordinator glue that
stays in `view.py`. `intents` is empty at Phase 1: the intent/command
registry is created in Phase 2 from the `_make_*` helpers.
